## 1. Introduction

The school shop delivery system is a software application designed to make it easier for students and staff to purchase items from shops within the school environment. The system will allow users to browse available products, place orders, make payments, and receive their purchased items at specific delivery locations provided within the school.

The purpose of creating this system is to reduce the high rate of long queues at shops when trying to purchase goods and items, limit the stress of students regularly visiting shops physically, and improve the convenience of shopping within the school.

## 2. Statement of Problem

Most students and staff often face difficulties when trying to purchase items from school shops, mostly when staff are busy with meetings and lecturers or when students are busy with academic activities, e.g. lectures, executive meetings (for students who hold positions at the student board), assignments, projects, etc.

These challenges include:

1. Experiencing difficulty when trying to purchase items during lectures or study periods.
2. It causes inconvenience and is most at times stressful for students and staff who don't want to physically visit shops to purchase items.
3. Lack of an organized system for students and staff to place or track their orders.
4. Long queues at school shops.
5. It leads to time wastage walking to shops to purchase items when you can just order them online and they can be delivered to you wherever you are on campus.

## 3. Objectives of the System

The objectives, also known as the benefits of the delivery system for school shops, are:

1. To reduce the time spent when physically visiting shops.
2. To enable users to view available products and their prices.
3. To ensure the availability of a convenient platform for students and staff to order products from the school shops.
4. To ensure that users, which are staff and students, are able to place and also track their orders.
5. To ensure that there's an active working delivery service within the school's environment.
6. To enable shop owners to manage products and users' orders more efficiently.

## 4. Target Users

The target users are:

1. **Students:** Students would be able to place orders, make payments, and track their orders until they get to the destination.
2. **Shop Owners:** Shop owners would be able to upload their products, update and review their prices, give discounts when needed, receive their customers' orders, and make incomes from both ends through the delivery system and physically.
3. **Delivery Personnel:** Delivery personnel would be able to view assigned orders, collect them, and deliver them to their respective locations. They also make a source of income from it.
4. **Administrator:** The administrator manages user accounts, monitors the activities going on in the system, and oversees the general operations of the system.

## 5. Functional Requirements

### 5.1 Customer (Student/Staff)

- **User registration and login:** Allows students and staff to create accounts and log in.
- **Browse shops:** Allows users to view different shops available on campus.
- **View products:** Displays available products, their descriptions, and prices.
- **Search and filter:** Enables users to search for specific products or shops.
- **Place orders:** Allows users to select products and place orders.
- **Make payments:** Supports available payment methods. It also enables the system to confirm successful payments.
- **Tracking of orders:** The system should allow users to track the status of their orders. It should also tell users when an item has been packed and is ready to be delivered.

### 5.2 Shop Management

- The system should allow shop owners to add, edit, and remove products.
- The system should allow shop owners to update product availability. If certain products or items are sold out, they should be able to notify users.

### 5.3 Delivery Personnel

- The system should allow delivery personnel to view assigned orders.
- The system should allow delivery personnel to update users on the status of their ordered items or goods.

## 6. Non-Functional Requirements

- **Security:** The system should be security-conscious in order to protect users' personal information as well as their payment details and avoid the risk of scams.
- **Performance:** The system should work efficiently without lagging or slow performance, ensuring that there is no traffic on the system and that it responds quickly when users browse products or place orders.
- **Reliability:** The system should ensure active consistency and a minimal error rate in order to enhance patronage from users.
- **Availability:** The system should be readily accessible during the school's official hours in order to ensure its efficiency.
- **Compatibility:** The system should work on smartphones, tablets, laptops, and computers (like the ones staff use in the office).
- **Usability:** The system should be user-friendly and simple to use.

## 7. Scope of the System

The system should focus on ordering and delivering products from shops located within the school environment. The system should include:

a. Users' registration and login.  
b. Browsing and searching of products.  
c. Shopping cart management (where users can pick the items they want to order).  
d. Order placements (where users place their orders).  
e. Payment confirmation (where users run through their items and confirm their payment/purchase).  
f. Tracking of orders (being able to know the state of the product during transportation and keeping tabs on the item until it arrives at the specific location given).  
g. Shop owners' product management.  
h. Delivery management (constantly checking on the delivery process until it has been delivered successfully).

## 8. Description Using a Flowchart

The document describes the process as the following sequence:

1. Student logs in.
2. Decides if They want to Order or be Delivery Personeeel.
3. Student browses for a product.
4. Student adds items to cart.
5. Student places an order and pays.
6. Shop/owner prepares the order.
7. Delivery personnel delivers the item.
8. Student/staff receives the order.

## 9. Assumptions and Limitations

### 9.1 Assumptions

1. We assume that students and staff should have access to smartphones.
2. We assume that school shops will provide enough accurate information on products and items that want to be purchased.
3. We assume that delivery personnel would be available during operating hours.
4. We assume users would provide accurate delivery locations to delivery personnel.

### 9.2 Limitations

1. The system is limited only to shops within the school premises and not outside the school.
2. Delivery is restricted/strictly for approved locations.
3. Delivery may be dependent/affected by the shops' operating hours.
4. The system requires an internet connection; without it, students and staff cannot access the system.

## 10. Expected Outcome

The expected outcome is to achieve a convenient and organized platform that will help improve shopping and delivery services within the school.

The system is also supposed to help reduce the number of queues at shops, save both students' and staff's time, improve how shop owners are able to manage orders, and ensure that the system is fast and more efficient in the delivery process.

## 11. Conclusion

The system is designed to improve the shopping experience within the school environment by introducing a digital platform for ordering and delivering products.

By ensuring that there are features such as browsing/searching for products, placing orders, payment for items purchased, and tracking of the delivery process from shops to the destination of arrival, the system aims to make shopping more convenient for students and staff while also helping school shop owners manage their businesses more efficiently.
